I’m Nikki, a Computer Engineering student at the University of Sulaimani.

I’m drawn to the space where software and hardware meet. I like understanding what happens beneath an interface as much as thinking about what a person sees and uses. For me, the fun is in asking good questions, exploring how a system fits together, and finding a useful way to bring an idea to life.
